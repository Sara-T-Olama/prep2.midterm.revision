🟢 Slide 2 • Number Systems

The Real Number Family

The Rule
  • Rational (\(\mathbb{Q}\)): Can be written as a fraction (Integers, terminating decimals, repeating decimals).
  • Irrational (\(\mathbb{Q}'\)): Cannot be a fraction (Non-perfect square roots, non-perfect cube roots, \(\pi\)).
  • Real (\(\mathbb{R}\)) = Rational \(\cup\) Irrational.
The Exam Trick
A square root is only rational if the number inside is a perfect square!
🏋️ Quick Practice: Classify \(-\sqrt{81}\) as Rational or Irrational.
Check inside the radical first!

🟢 Slide 3 • Inequalities & Intervals

Intervals & The Number Line

The Rule
  • \([a, b]\) = Closed (Includes both ends)
  • \((a, b)\) = Open (Excludes both ends)
  • \([a, b)\) = Half-open
The Exam Trick
Infinity always gets a round bracket! You can never touch infinity, so it is never closed. Example: \((-\infty, 5]\).
🏋️ Quick Practice: Write the interval for: "All real numbers greater than or equal to \(-5\)."
Infinity always wears a round hat!

🟢 Slide 4 • Radicals

Radicals & Roots (The Golden Rule)

The Rule
You can only add/subtract roots if they are similar (same index, same radicand).
Example: \(\sqrt{2} + 5\sqrt{2} = 6\sqrt{2}\).
The Exam Trick
You CANNOT split a root over addition or subtraction! \(\sqrt{a + b} \neq \sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}\).
🏋️ Quick Practice: Simplify \(\sqrt{16 + 9}\)
Add inside first! Don't split roots!

🟢 Slide 5 • Exponent Laws

Exponents (The Negative Trap)

The Rule
  • \(a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}\) (Add)
  • \(a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}\) (Subtract)
  • \((a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}\) (Multiply)
  • \(a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}\)
The Exam Trick
Watch the brackets with negative numbers! \((-4)^2 = 16\) vs. \(-4^2 = -16\).
🏋️ Quick Practice: Evaluate \(2^{-3}\)
Negative exponent flips the base!

🟢 Slide 6 • Factoring

Factoring Part 1 (GCF & Difference of Squares)

The Rule
  • GCF: Always pull out the greatest common factor first!
  • Difference of Squares: \(a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)\).
The Exam Trick
Students forget GCF! Example: \(2x^2 - 32 = 2(x^2 - 16) = 2(x - 4)(x + 4)\).
🏋️ Quick Practice: Factor \(x^2 - 121\)
Always check for GCF first!
🤖

🟢 Slide 7 • Factoring

Factoring Part 2 (Trinomials & Perfect Squares)

The Rule
\(x^2 + bx + c = (x + m)(x + n)\) where \(m \times n = c\) and \(m + n = b\).
Perfect Square: \(a^2 + 2ab + b^2 = (a + b)^2\).
The Exam Trick
If the middle term is twice the product of square roots of outer terms, it's a perfect square!
🏋️ Quick Practice: Factor \(x^2 + 9x + 20\)
Find factors adding to 9, multiplying to 20!
🧠

🟢 Slide 8 • Factoring

Factoring Part 3 (Sum & Difference of Cubes)

The Rule
\(a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2)\)
\(a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)\)
S.O.A.P. Trick
Same sign, Opposite sign, Always Positive!
🏋️ Quick Practice: Factor \(x^3 - 125\)
Remember S.O.A.P. for cubes!
