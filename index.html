🟢 Slide 2 • Unit 1 - Ratio & Proportion (2 minutes)

The Golden Rule: Cross Multiply!

Cross Multiplication Rule
\(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}\) means that \(a \times d = b \times c\).
Multiply the extremes and the means to verify if two ratios are proportional!
Teacher Script: "If you want to check if two ratios make a proportion, just cross-multiply. Multiply the extremes and the means. If they are equal, it's a proportion."
🏋️ Quick Check: Are these ratios proportional? \(\frac{5}{8}\) and \(\frac{15}{24}\)
Extremes = Means!

🟢 Slide 3 • Unit 1 - Scale Drawings (2 minutes)

Scale Drawings & Unit Conversion

The Golden Rule
Map Distance \(\times\) Scale = Actual Distance
The Trap: Watch the units!
To go from cm to km, divide by 100,000 (or move the decimal point 5 places left).
Teacher Script: "The math is easy, but the units will trick you. 3 cm times 400,000 is 1,200,000 cm. Now, jump 5 decimal places to get kilometers."
🏋️ Quick Check: Map scale is \(1 : 400,000\). Distance on map = \(3\text{ cm}\). Real distance in km?
Move decimal 5 places left!

🟢 Slide 4 • Unit 1 - Proportional Division (2 minutes)

Proportional Division: Value of 1 Part

The Golden Rule
Find the value of 1 part first!
Step 1: Total Amount \(\div\) Total Parts (Sum of ratio numbers) = 1 Part.
Step 2: Multiply 1 Part by the person's ratio share.
Teacher Script: "Add the ratio numbers: 3 plus 5 is 8 parts total. Divide 1,200 by 8. That's 150 per part. Sara gets 5 parts, so 5 times 150."
🏋️ Quick Check: Divide 1,200 EGP between Ahmed and Sara in ratio \(3 : 5\). How much does Sara get?
Always sum the parts first!

🟢 Slide 5 • Unit 1 - Percentages (2 minutes)

Percentage Increase & Discount

The Golden Rule
For an increase of \(x\%\): Multiply by \(\left(1 + \frac{x}{100}\right)\).
For a decrease of \(x\%\): Multiply by \(\left(1 - \frac{x}{100}\right)\).
Teacher Script: "Don't calculate the discount and subtract—that takes too long! If there is a 15% discount, you are paying 85% of the price. Multiply 2000 by 0.85."
🏋️ Quick Check: A phone costs 2,000 EGP with a 15% discount. What is the new price?
100% - 15% = 85%!

🟢 Slide 6 • Unit 1 - Sets & Venn Diagrams (3 minutes)

Sets & Venn Diagrams

The Golden Rule
Fill in the intersection first!
Formula: \(n(A \cup B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cap B)\)
Teacher Script: "The intersection is the 8 who play both. Subtract 8 from 20 (12 football only) and 8 from 18 (10 basketball only). Total who play at least one = 12 + 10 + 8 = 30. The class has 35. So, 5 play neither."
🏋️ Quick Check: In a class of 35 students, 20 play football, 18 play basketball, and 8 play both. How many play neither?
Start with the middle overlap!

🟢 Slide 7 • Unit 1 - Integers & Rational Numbers (2 minutes)

Integers & Rational Numbers

The Golden Rule
  • Sign Rules: Same signs = Positive. Different signs = Negative.
  • Rational Numbers: Fraction \(\frac{a}{b}\) where \(b \neq 0\). (Note: \(\pi\) and non-perfect roots like \(\sqrt{2}\) are NOT rational).
Teacher Script: "Negative times negative is positive, so that's 24. Then add negative 10. 24 minus 10 is 14."
🏋️ Quick Check: Calculate \(-6 \times (-4) + (-10)\)
(-) × (-) = (+)

🟢 Slide 8 • Unit 2 - Algebra: Expressions & Formulas (3 minutes)

Algebra: Like Terms & Substitution

The Golden Rule
You can ONLY add or subtract Like Terms (same variable and same power).
Teacher Script: "For the first one, group x's: 8x - 2x = 6x, and 3 + 7 = 10. For substitution with y=4: 3 × (4 squared) = 3 × 16 = 48. NOT 12 squared!"
🏋️ Quick Check 1: Simplify \(8x + 3 - 2x + 7\)  |  Quick Check 2: If \(y = 4\), what is \(3y^2\)?
Square first, then multiply!

🟢 Slide 9 • Unit 2 - Linear Equations (3 minutes)

Solving Linear Equations

The Golden Rule
Do the Opposite (Inverse Operations) to isolate the variable.
Order: Undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division.
Teacher Script: "The x is multiplied by 4, then 9 is subtracted. Work backwards: opposite of -9 is +9. 15+9=24. Then divide by 4: x = 6."
🏋️ Quick Check: Solve for \(x\): \(4x - 9 = 15\)
Undo operations in reverse!
